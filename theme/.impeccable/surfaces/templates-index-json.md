# Surface brief · Home «El túnel de estrellas»

Scope: Shopify home (theme/templates/index.json, layout fe-cosmos, fe-* sections). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: couples 25–45 in Spain buying an emotional gift, mostly on mobile. Action: design a lámina and buy (from 4,95 €). Proof: the sky is computed; every example lámina is a real date and city. Constraints: no fake proof, keep placeholders, everything editable in the theme editor, first paint < 2.5 s, reduced-motion still.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One continuous cinematic shot the visitor drives with the scroll — a gold point, a nebula tunnel, the sun, Earth at night, the real sky of one night — that ends as the lámina floating in 3D. It refuses the category default of a product grid with a hero banner and a star-map mockup on a wall.

OWN-WORLD: Near-monochrome deep space (#05070D, #0B1426) with gold only as light (sun, city lights, constellation lines) and ivory type. Frosted glass panels (white 6 %, blur 18px, 1px 12 % border, 16px radius), pill navigation inside one glass capsule, one small pale-blue pill button. Headlines: Inter Tight 300 line + Instrument Serif italic line, very large, tight leading.

STORY: Visitor feels the night was unique, learns the sky in the lámina is the real one for their date and place, sees the price from 4,95 € and free shipping, designs it live, picks a format, buys.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed black film canvas; a single breathing gold point at centre; bottom-left two-line headline «Hubo una noche / que lo cambió todo.» at ~6.5vw desktop, ~11vw mobile; bottom-right a 3-line paragraph and the pale-blue «Crear mi lámina →» pill; top: logo left, glass pill nav + blue button right; mobile: fixed glass buy bar with «Desde 4,95 €».

FORM: Scroll-scrubbed cinematic tunnel (reference: motionsites «Frozen Cave» technique), live Three.js on desktop, pre-rendered frame pack on mobile. seed: none (pinned) — «Un único plano cinematográfico a pantalla completa en el que la cámara avanza hacia el fondo de un túnel según haces scroll».

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
